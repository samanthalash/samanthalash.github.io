# Current Lessons

- Preserve working history by isolating the new portfolio instead of refactoring
  the archive in place.
- Separate content authority from interaction authority: Samantha's goal exports
  control words and visual hierarchy; Constanza's site controls browsing behavior.
- Durable documentation should record current truth, while narrative logs explain
  how that truth was learned.
- Responsive fidelity means preserving hierarchy and character, not shrinking a
  desktop canvas until it becomes unreadable.
